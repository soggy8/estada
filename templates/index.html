Behind Estada

Founded by Andrej Trendov

Andrej is a full-stack developer and designer who started building websites at 10. He works in Python, Flask, FastAPI, and React, and also builds electronics with Arduino and ESP32. He won a national hackathon, holds a full scholarship to FINKI in North Macedonia, and placed first in the national Kengur competition.

How We Work

How a site gets made

01

Talk

You describe the venue or the product. Andrej writes back with what the site needs to show.

02

Design

The page, the menu or the product story, and the clips when the site needs video.

03

Build

The site is built and put on a server Estada runs. You can open it before it is public.

04

Launch

The domain goes live. Later changes, a new menu item or a new clip, come to the same person.
